About the college

A legacy built on the lamp of learning.

The seal's torch and rays represent the light of knowledge the College of Education has passed on since 1947 — the laurel branches mark the achievement of every graduate who has carried it into a classroom.

SED Connect Global keeps that same spirit accessible: a public hub — no accounts required — where prospective and current students, cooperating schools, and faculty can find what they need about the college at a glance.
