Ways to go global

Exchange Programs.

From a full semester abroad to a short immersion, there's a track for wherever students are in the program.

Semester Track

Student Teaching Exchange

Full-term practicum placement at a partner school abroad, supervised jointly by SED and the host institution's faculty. Open to 4th-year education majors in good academic standing.

[1 semester]
Faculty Track

Faculty Exchange & Research Collaboration

Co-teaching, guest lecturing, and joint research placements for SED faculty at partner universities, with reciprocal visiting-faculty slots for our own campus.

[4–8 weeks]
Short Track

Summer Immersion & Study Tour

A short, guided trip pairing classroom observation with cultural immersion — built for students who want international exposure without a full-term commitment.

[2–3 weeks]
