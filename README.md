# SED Connect Global

SED Connect Global is a centralized **One-Stop Shop** platform designed to streamline access to academic resources, institutional services, and internationalization opportunities for the School of Education. Built with **Google Sites**, the platform serves as a single gateway that connects students, faculty, and staff to existing university systems while improving accessibility, communication, and information management.

## Project Overview

SED Connect Global addresses the challenge of fragmented information by consolidating essential academic and administrative resources into one accessible website. Rather than replacing existing institutional systems, the platform integrates with services such as the **Learning Management System (LMS)**, **Student Portal**, and **EduDrive**, providing a more convenient and organized user experience.

## Features

- 🌐 **Centralized Academic Hub**
  - Access academic resources and institutional services from a single platform.

- 📢 **Official Announcements**
  - Receive verified announcements published directly through the website.

- 📅 **Events Calendar**
  - Stay updated on academic, institutional, and international events.

- 🌍 **Internationalization Hub**
  - Explore student exchange programs, scholarships, global partnerships, and international opportunities.

- 📚 **CHED Memorandum Repository**
  - Browse organized archives of official CHED memoranda and related documents.

- 🔗 **Integrated System Access**
  - Quick links to the LMS, Student Portal, EduDrive, and other university services.

- 📱 **Responsive Design**
  - Optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

## Security & Governance

The platform is designed with sustainability and security in mind through:

- Institutional email authentication
- Two-factor authentication (2FA) for administrators
- Role-based editing permissions
- Content review and approval workflow
- Monthly website backups
- Turnover documentation and administrator manuals
- Annual continuity training for website managers

## Project Objectives

- Centralize academic resources and institutional services.
- Improve access to official announcements and university information.
- Support internationalization initiatives and global engagement.
- Reduce information fragmentation across multiple platforms.
- Maintain a secure, reliable, and sustainable website management system.

## Technology Stack

- **Platform:** Google Sites
- **Storage:** Google Drive / EduDrive
- **Authentication:** Google Workspace Institutional Accounts
- **Integrations:**
  - Learning Management System (LMS)
  - Student Portal
  - EduDrive
  - Google Calendar
  - Google Forms

## Target Users

- Students
- Faculty Members
- School Administrators
- International Partners
- Prospective Students

## Vision

SED Connect Global aims to become the School of Education's official digital gateway by providing a secure, accessible, and centralized platform that enhances communication, supports academic excellence, and promotes international collaboration through efficient access to institutional resources and services.
