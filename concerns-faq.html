Who can submit a concern or question?

+

Anyone — current students, faculty, alumni, or visitors to this site. The form is open to everyone, not just enrolled BSEd or BSNEd students.

Is my submission anonymous?

+

The form may ask for contact details so the College of Education can follow up with you directly. Check the fields on the form itself for what's required versus optional.

How long until I get a response?

+

Response times vary by the nature of the concern. For urgent matters, reach out directly at sedconnectglobal@gmail.com in addition to submitting the form.
