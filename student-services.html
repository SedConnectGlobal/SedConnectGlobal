Student services and forms

Everything you need to request, in one place.

A directory of the offices SED students turn to most, plus the forms they issue — gathered here so you don't have to hunt across pages or social media for the right one.

Student services

University Registrar

Enrollment records, transcripts, certifications, and academic document requests.

Guidance & Counseling (SDPC)

Counseling services, excuse slip processing, and student welfare concerns.

Scholarships & Financial Assistance

Academic, leadership, and needs-based scholarship applications and renewals.

University Clinic

Medical and dental consultations, health certificates, and wellness referrals.

Office of Student Affairs

Student organization accreditation, discipline concerns, and ID & permit processing.

USJ-R Library

Book loans, digital library databases, and research assistance.
